Spring Family Night

Join the Lincoln Elementary community for an evening of hands-on learning, food, and fun for the whole family. Explore STEAM activity stations, enjoy a picnic dinner on the lawn, browse our spring book fair, and enter the raffle for some great prizes. We can't wait to celebrate spring together with our students and their families!

Friday, May 30, 2026  •  5:30 PM – 8:00 PM  •  Lincoln Elementary Multipurpose Room

Free to attend. Light dinner provided — please register so we can plan accordingly.

Space is limited! Registration closes Thursday, May 29 at 5:00 PM.

Spring Family Night is one of our most popular events of the year, bringing together hundreds of students, parents, and staff. Please complete the short registration form below so we can make sure there's enough food and seating for everyone.

What to Expect

An evening built around our students. Wander between activity stations, grab dinner with neighbors, and stick around for the raffle. Younger siblings are welcome too — there will be a craft corner just for them.
